Live As You Like

in build

You live people of one place, one after another. What you decide as the first reaches the second as rumour: bent a step, your name rubbed out. Then you are the one it reached. One fixed rule does the bending, and no language model is involved.

One decision, three views of it

WHAT YOU DIDthe act itself, in one sentence, with no judgement attachedyou saw it happen
HOW IT LEFTthe same act as the street now carries itfear up a step, your name gone
WHAT ARRIVEDwhat the next person hears before they decide anythingand now you are them

The bending is one fixed rule the engine applies, not a writer's flourish and not a model's improvisation. Twelve years later the last of them rides in with the same paper the first one carried, remembering the beginning wrong.

Two shapes, both built and played

THE CHAINfour people of one settlement, lived one after another, top to bottombuilt 15 August
THE RAIDone body instead of four, and one night cut into three beats — evening, night, daybreakbuilt 16 August

Which of the two the game becomes is not decided yet. The rule for deciding is written down and it is short: play it, and say whether it pulls. If it does not, the shape is dropped whole in an evening — no new mechanism is brought in to rescue it.

What is deliberately absent

NO PSYCHOLOGYthe model of need and scar runs the engine and never reaches the screenpeople, things, circumstances
NO MODELthe truth is held by the engine; nothing is generated while you playa deduction cannot depend on it
NO VERDICTnobody in the chain is a villain, and the game states no conclusionthe player draws it or doesn't

Where the shape came from

PAPERS, PLEASETaken apart layer by layer — mechanics, idea, subtraction. Seven forks the author faced, and morality without moralising, technicallyin Russian
