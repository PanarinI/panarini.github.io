What it prints

How it works

You choose the sound being practised and tick the sounds this child cannot say yet. Everything after that is automatic. The dictionary is cut down to words that hold the target sound and none of the forbidden ones, and seven blocks are assembled onto one page: warm-up, the sound alone, syllables, words, a grammar game, sentences, a tongue-twister. The same bank feeds every other material on the ladder.

Every word is transcribed by sound, not by spelling, because a Russian word can be written with one consonant and spoken with another. If a word cannot be verified, the build stops.

What the engine learned

19 Augpictures

Three words were dropped after the author went through the drawings one by one. A “ten” had been drawn as the figure 10 — a sign, not an object; a diminutive of “newspaper” cannot be told from a newspaper by a drawing at all. A picture on this material has to name an object, and if the drawing cannot name it, the word leaves rather than the picture being redrawn twice.

18 Augthe picture bank closed

Every word the maze can put on paper now has a drawing of its own — in colour and in black and white, from the same original. The check runs across the whole pool, for all sounds and positions, and returns zero words without a picture. Bought stock pictures had been failing on exactly the thing the material needs: the child must name the object with the word we filtered for, and a stock drawing of a “market” is as often called a shop.

18 Augthe ladder became the screen

The materials used to sit in a flat row, and choosing one meant knowing the engine. They now stand by step of automation — one sound, syllables, words, word pairs, text — which is how a therapist already thinks about the child. The three that are not built keep their place in the row and say “later”: an empty step is information too.

10 Augtwo exercises existed on paper and not in fact

Both were listed as working features. Neither could be built for almost any sheet: the rule they stood on had been switched off two days earlier, and the table replacing it covered twenty-three words out of seventeen hundred. They were counted as existing without existing. Once the tables were filled, one builds on twenty-eight sheets out of twenty-nine, the other on fourteen.

10 Augone exercise cannot be built for hissing sounds, and the reason is the language

The affectionate form almost always carries one particular consonant. On a sheet for any hissing or whistling sound it is forbidden: a child practising one slides into the other. Sixteen words on such a sheet; forms exist for twelve; eight of those fall at the purity gate. No engineering moves that number, so the screen says it plainly instead of reporting that the exercise “could not be assembled”.

9 Augten tests had never run, and the suite reported all green

The single line that tells a file to execute its tests was sitting in the middle of that file instead of at the end. Everything below it was silently skipped: ten test functions, five written by other people. The suite still printed a clean result. Moving one line took the count from 1224 checks to 1649.

9 Auga finished block was measured and parked, because the page was already full

Word pairs were built as another block of the sheet. Then the sheet was measured: an A4 page holds 273 millimetres of material, and the existing blocks already asked for 305 to 363. The block became a separate printable material instead. The sheet itself did not change by a millimetre; that was checked against the previous version.

8 Augverbs were kept out of sentences until the dictionary knew enough about them

Sentences on the sheet were frozen phrases: point at a picture, name it. Making them live needs verbs. A verb cannot be placed without knowing whether its subject is animate and whether the action is finished, and neither follows from how the word ends. The verbs waited while three properties were marked by hand across the dictionary. Then the sentences moved: from “here is a ram” to “an elk is swimming past”.

8 Augthree materials were teaching the wrong sound

A practice track for one voiced consonant printed syllables that come out as its voiceless twin when read aloud. The sheet was training the opposite sound. Two more materials had the same class of fault. All three came off the paper the day it was measured. What a letter looks like and what a mouth does with it are different facts, and only the second is allowed to decide anything.

6 Augthe engine was describing a child it had never met

The sheet printed a line about sounds that were “still difficult” for this child. Nothing in the input says that: the therapist ticks which sounds to avoid, which is not the same as a diagnosis. The line came off. A generated document may state what it did, never what it inferred about a person.

6 Auga guess of ours became “canon” three files later

An assumption was written into a research note, then cited into a second, and by the third it had the authority of a source. It was implemented as though the field required it. The rule now: a confidence mark belongs to the quotation, not to the conclusion drawn from it, however obvious that conclusion looks.

6 Augprinted material is looked at before it is shown, not after

Three times a build was reported as done because the tests passed. Three times the printed page was visibly broken: lines not starting where they should, a scrawl instead of a track, a letter running off the edge. Tests pass on the data; paper is a different medium. The screenshot is now taken before the work is handed over.

4 Auga believed rule about how therapists work was disproved by their own plans

Navigation rested on the idea that one session equals one step of difficulty. Ten real lesson plans were coded into a dataset of thirty-three task names, and not one worked that way. A therapist works from how far this child has come with this sound, and builds a run-up to it. The idea was dropped; the engine, tested against the same dataset, held.

3 Augfive untruths came off the sheet

The sheet claimed more than the data could support — the source of a block, what a child had mastered, which manual an exercise came from. Provenance is now computed from the data, not declared. A claim without a traceable source counts as a defect.

3 Augthe user's words are kept verbatim, in a file of their own

A speech therapist described the format she needed. What went into the code was my summary of her words, not the words. A week later the two were compared: the format in the code was not hers. Her words now live untouched in their own file, and any reading of them sits beside them, never on top.

The generator runs in a browser and needs no installation; the source code is closed. No sheet has been used with a child in a real session yet: everything so far is checked against literature, data and the plans of practising therapists, not against practice itself.
