Richie

in build

Reachy Mini is a ready-made robot by Pollen Robotics. We bought it assembled. Inside there are nine servo motors, four microphones, a speaker, a camera and a single-board computer, plus the maker's own program that drives the motors. It arrives able to move and to run apps from the maker's store. It does not hold a conversation. That is the work here. Two programs were written for it. The one inside the robot takes the sound from the microphone and sends it to a cloud model, which recognises speech and answers by voice. The same program plays that answer through the speaker. The one on the laptop reads the transcript after a pause and writes what matters into a text file, which is how Richie remembers yesterday. It also searches the web when he asks, while he goes on talking.

What was bought and what was written

BODYnine servo motors, four microphones, a speaker, a camera, a single-board computer. The maker's program drives the motors; whether they are on is shown only by their own status, and its readiness flag stays off even on a robot that movesbought ready-made
CONVERSATIONtakes the sound from the microphone, sends it to the cloud model and plays the answer through the speaker. The laptop takes no part in this, and the model is used on a free tokenour program, inside the robot
MEMORY AND SEARCHreads the transcript after a pause and writes what matters into the robot's text file; on request it searches the web and reads files on the laptop. This is the only part we pay forour program, on the laptop

The three are started and stopped by three separate buttons, because they break separately: the robot can answer out loud while its motors are off. There is one dependency — the conversation program does not start when the motors are off.

Journal

10 Augdocumentation

We built a test rig to work out the direction a voice comes from and recorded twelve scenes. It turned out that the robot does not tell a sound in front from a sound behind. This is written in the maker's documentation: the four microphones sit in one row, so the direction is computed within 180° only. Half a day went into checking a known fact. Since then a new task starts with reading the documentation and the existing apps, and measurements are made only where there is no data.

9 Augsearch

When Richie was asked something he had to look up, he went silent for the whole search. The program inside the robot was waiting for the laptop and could not finish the reply. Now the request is confirmed at once. Richie says that he is searching and goes on talking, and the result arrives a few seconds later as a separate line. The cost of this: the answer is not attached to the question and can arrive when the conversation has moved on.

9 Augcost

Requests to the model cost 10 dollars in one day. One question was processed by six requests in a row. Each of them sent the whole previous conversation together with the text of the pages found. The longer the conversation, the more every next request costs. We split the jobs — search goes to the more expensive model, sorting the conversation into memory goes to the cheap one. Every answer now prints its price. After the switch the cheap model turned out not to accept one of the parameters, and memory quietly stopped being written for a day.

7 Augmemory

Richie was reading the project files, the ones where we write down the work and the breakdowns. He was telling the author about faults he no longer had. We made separate files for the robot itself: who he is, what works right now, why things are done this way. He reads them before every answer. The cost: we update these files by hand, he does not keep them himself.

6 Augmicrophone

The microphone hands over the recording in two channels. We were joining them into one row, one after the other. The recording played twice as fast, and speech recognition returned fragments or nothing at all. The level and the spectrum looked normal, so the error was invisible on the instruments. We started taking the average of the two channels: speech was recognised, and the background noise we had taken for the motors dropped elevenfold.

4 Augcontrol

Controlling the robot through the maker's library did not work. It drops the connection if the robot does not respond for a second, and the robot does not respond while it is busy. Its video channel hangs the whole interface. We went to the service program inside the robot directly: it has a web interface of its own, and a movement command runs in 23 milliseconds. What we take from this: if a device comes with a convenient wrapper, it is worth looking at what is under it.

4 Augassembly

The robot was ordered in July and arrived in a box; assembly took one evening. Out of the box it moves, and apps from the maker's store run on it. We ran three of them to see what the platform can do. None of them makes a conversation that keeps its history, so from here on the work is our own programs.

Richie stands at home, the programs written for him are not published and the source code is closed. This page describes what works today; the camera, echo cancellation and movements of his own are not done yet.
