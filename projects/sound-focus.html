Sound Focus

in use

A focus timer with no digits on screen. Time is made of sound: it rises as you begin, holds steady through the session, and eases off as you arrive. Nothing is streamed and nothing is played back — every sound is computed on the device while you work.

Who it is for

The position is one sentence: it accompanies, it does not punish. Attention is not missing, it is hard to regulate — which makes two moments hard, not one: getting in, and being torn out. A timer that forces the start and then breaks the flow with a bell gets both of them wrong.

Decision log

Newest first. A decision appears here only if a reader with no context can follow it to the end without once asking “what is that?” — the rest stay in the project’s own log.

2026-08-07The tone knob ships at zero, for everyone

The product promises warm noise, and a chord offered instead is not what anyone came for. An earlier version put the knob at the centre for new users only — but a newcomer cannot be told from a long-time user by the absence of a setting, because the setting is new and neither of them has one. The split went out together with the idea behind it.

2026-08-06The tonal layer rides the same rails as the noise, not beside them

On the bench the layer went straight to the output, which was right for judging it on its own. In the product that would drift apart: on pause the noise would stop while the tone kept playing; at the end the noise would thin while the tone stood still. One output, one set of rails — volume, pause, fade and the inaudible keep-alive floor now apply to everything at once. The layer is also told which phase the session is in, because music that sounds the same at the start and at the finish cancels the point of arriving.

2026-08-06A finished feature was turned down the evening it started working

Ten milestones laid along the flight, receding by the same perspective the starfield already uses, so the remaining distance could be read in space rather than in numbers. It was built, it worked, and it was rejected that evening: the sidebar reads well enough without it. The lesson was mine rather than the feature’s — the choice had been offered as “what to build next” and was read, correctly, as “what goes into this release”.

2026-08-06Presence is carried by the toolbar icon, not by a tab injected into other people’s pages

An edge tab on every page costs a scripting permission, an optional host permission and a reviewer’s attention, and it buys one thing: a door back. The icon is visible on any tab without touching a single page, and clicking it already opens the panel. The drawing law came with it — show the distance still ahead, never the time draining away: no digits, no red, no acceleration toward the finish. A countdown in permanent view presses; an arc of what is left does not.

2026-08-06Packaging is a script with a whitelist, never a hand-made archive

Two files are attached loosely, so a missing one does not crash the extension — it ships silently without its icon, or without its tone, and would pass review in that state. The script fails if a file is absent from disk or from the finished archive, and it refuses to overwrite an archive that already exists, because that version may be the one already in the store.

2026-08-04A second sound is a bench experiment, not a release feature — and never a library of sounds

The noise field is a market of sleep apps and hardware; entering it as a feature means becoming the fiftieth noise generator with nothing to tell it apart. But individual variance in what people can work under is real and measured, so one timbre cannot fit everyone. The fork is therefore worked out on a bench driving the real engine through its ordinary controls, and a control in the panel has to earn its place before it appears.

2026-08-01The worst bug was proved by a symptom, not by a reproduction

“Turn the volume to zero, wait, turn it back — no sound.” Zero volume is real silence at the output, and the browser keeps an audio document alive only while sound is actually playing, so it was being shut down and taking the session state with it. The cure is an inaudible floor on the master gain, around −80 dB and below the sixteen-bit quantum, held for as long as a session exists. It treats the cause; restoring a lost session would have treated the symptom and risked resuming a flight from the middle.

2026-07-25The ember was abandoned for a flight toward a star

An ember sitting in a panel was rejected in four words — a potato with a rim. What replaced it was not a repaint: the ring went, time became a slider under the scene, and energy became the speed you travel at. Everything the old skin implied about the hour of the day went with it, because a flight does not happen at a time of day.

The extension is in the Chrome Web Store as Minimalist Timer. How it is listed and advertised — the wording, the keywords, the campaign and its numbers — stays off this page.
